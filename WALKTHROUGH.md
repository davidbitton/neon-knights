# Neon Knights: walkthrough

**Spoilers below.** This is the shortest route to the full 80 points, followed
by every death in the game. If you only want a nudge, type `hint` in the game
instead: it gives three progressively less subtle hints per puzzle.

The puzzle chain in one sentence: impress the bartender to get courage
(a drink), compliment the hot dog lady to get a hot dog, feed it to the mime
so he steps off the storm grate, fish five dollars out of the grate with your
comb, spend it on a carnation and breath mints, wear the carnation past the
doorman, and serenade Vonda with fresh breath. That wins her over but not the job: the
second night starts there. Vonda's midnight gown is locked in Dryer 3 at the
laundromat. Get Dee's briefing with the claim ticket Vonda gives you, borrow a
rod on the pier, comb the motel's night manager out of the way so you can take
a shrimp, catch a flounder, trade it to the pelican for the dryer key, and carry
the gown back up to the Crow's Nest.

Most actions need Chip standing next to the thing. Clicking a hotspot with a
verb walks him there. If you type a command from too far away, the game tells
you to get closer.

## 1. The Rusty Anchor (20 points)

| Command | Points |
|---|---|
| `take matchbook` (on the bar) | 5 |
| `read matchbook`: Vonda says to ask Gus for the Special, then meet her at the Crow's Nest | 3 |
| `use quarter on jukebox`: Gus won't mix the Special without music | 5 |
| `ask gus about the special` | 5 |
| `drink special`: the courage you'll need on the roof | 2 |

Leave by the front door (walk into the doorway on the right, or type `leave`).

## 2. The Boardwalk (5 points)

| Command | Points |
|---|---|
| `look through telescope` (at the east end of the railing): you spot Vonda on the casino roof | 2 |
| `talk to dot` (optional; ask a few times): she mentions the starving mime in the alley and the doorman, Tiny | 0 |
| `compliment dot` (or `flirt with dot`): she gives you a free hot dog | 3 |

Walk off the **west** (left) edge of the screen into the alley.

## 3. Back Alley (10 points)

| Command | Points |
|---|---|
| `give hot dog to mime`: he finally steps out of his invisible box, and off the storm grate | 5 |
| `use comb on grate`: you fish out a soggy $5 bill | 5 |

Walk off the **east** (right) edge to get back to the boardwalk, then go
through the middle door (the Lucky Gull 24/7).

## 4. Lucky Gull 24/7 (6 points)

Stand at the counter by Brenda.

| Command | Points |
|---|---|
| `buy carnation` ($3) | 3 |
| `buy mints` ($2) | 3 |

Leave through the door at the bottom of the screen and enter the Golden Clam,
the casino at the east end.

## 5. The Golden Clam (7 points)

| Command | Points |
|---|---|
| `wear carnation` (this works anywhere) | 2 |
| `talk to tiny`: "That flower is doing a lot of work, friend. Go on up." | 5 |

Walk into the elevator, or type `take elevator`.

## 6. The Crow's Nest (14 points now, 2 more at the end)

| Command | Points |
|---|---|
| `eat mints` (this works anywhere): your breath is now legally a cold front | 2 |
| `sing to vonda` (or `talk to vonda`): the song itself | 10 |
| (the same scene) Vonda hands you a laundry claim ticket | 2 |

You have to have drunk the Special first, or Chip only squeaks. You also have
to have eaten the mints, or she'll say your breath smells like bilge water.
This is no longer the end of the game. Vonda's gold midnight gown is stuck in
Dryer 3 at Suds & Duds, and you're now at 62 of 80.

## 7. Suds & Duds (5 points)

From the alley, walk off the **west** (left) edge of the screen, or type
`go to laundromat`.

| Command | Points |
|---|---|
| `give ticket to dee` (or `show ticket to dee`): she tells you the key is in the pelican's pouch, and what Percival likes | 2 |
| `use key on dryer`, later, once you have the key: Dryer 3 coughs up the gown | 3 |

Once it's open, don't `climb into dryer` (see deaths). Leave east, back into the alley.

## 8. Gull Point Pier (9 points)

From the boardwalk, walk off the **east** (right) edge, or type `go to pier`.

| Command | Points |
|---|---|
| `take rod` (leaning on the bait shack) | 2 |
| `use rod` at the railing, with the shrimp in your pocket as bait: you hook a flounder | 3 |
| `give flounder to pelican`: Percival swaps the dryer key for it | 4 |

Percival patrols back and forth along the deck, and you have to be standing next
to him when you give him the fish. He won't take the shrimp. He wants the
flounder. The flounder needs the rod *and* the shrimp (as bait), so fetch the
rod first, then go to the motel for the shrimp, and come back to fish. The
pelican only hands over the key once Dee has briefed you on him.

## 9. The Sandpiper Motor Court (2 points)

From the pier, walk off the **east** edge.

| Command | Points |
|---|---|
| `use comb on ferris` (or `give comb to ferris`): Chip fixes her ruined bouffant, and she heads for the restroom to admire it, leaving the cart unattended | 0 |
| `take shrimp` (the complimentary breakfast cart, "GUESTS ONLY") | 2 |

You keep the comb. Walk off the **west** edge to get back to the pier.

## The finale

Go back through the pier, the boardwalk and the alley to the laundromat for
`use key on dryer` (3 points, you now hold the gown, 78 of 80). Then take the
gown all the way back to the Crow's Nest:

| Command | Points |
|---|---|
| `give gown to vonda` | 2 |

The ending plays and shows your final score: **80 of 80, "Legend of the Second
Night."**

## Full command list, in order

All 80 points, in one run, assuming you start at the Rusty Anchor:

1. Bar: `take matchbook`, `read matchbook`, `use quarter on jukebox`, `ask gus about the special`, `drink special`
2. Boardwalk: `look through telescope`, `compliment dot`
3. Alley: `give hot dog to mime`, `use comb on grate`
4. Store: `buy carnation`, `buy mints`
5. Casino: `wear carnation`, `talk to tiny`
6. Rooftop: `eat mints`, `sing to vonda`
7. Laundromat: `give ticket to dee`
8. Pier: `take rod`
9. Motel: `use comb on ferris`, `take shrimp`
10. Pier: `use rod`, `give flounder to pelican`
11. Laundromat: `use key on dryer`
12. Rooftop: `give gown to vonda`

## Every way to die

Save first. Each death gets its own tombstone, cause and epitaph.

| Where | Command | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Rusty Anchor | `eat egg`, twice (Gus warns you the first time) | Death by pickled egg |
| Back Alley | `climb into dumpster` (it's 11:59 and pickup is at midnight) | Collected with the trash |
| Lucky Gull | `drink slushie` | Brain freeze, extra large |
| Golden Clam | `play slots` while carrying any money (even your lucky quarter) | Buried by a jackpot |
| Crow's Nest | `dive into pool` (it's drained) | A swan dive into nothing |
| Suds & Duds | `climb into dryer` (once you've unlocked it: it's warm and it looks cozy) | Tumble dried on high |
| Anywhere | `eat shrimp`: with the shrimp in your pocket, or straight off the cart at the motel | Complimentary shrimp |
